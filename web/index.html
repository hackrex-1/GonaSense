Data and limits

  • Rainfall, temperature and root-zone wetness come from NASA POWER daily estimates, not ground stations.
  • POWER's grid is coarser than an LGA, so some neighbouring LGAs share the same modelled series.
  • Each location is represented by one LGA centroid, not a farmer's field.
  • Scenarios are a sensitivity test, not a forecast.
  • Crop thresholds marked "unverified estimate" still need local expert review.
  • This is a decision aid. Your experience and your extension officer's advice come first.
